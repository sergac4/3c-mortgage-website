Your report uses available property information and owner-supplied figures. Estimates are not an appraisal, a cash offer, a loan approval or a guarantee. Sale proceeds are shown before income taxes. The report is not legal, tax or investment advice. If tax consequences, tenancy law or legal rights are part of your decision, 3C may recommend that you consult the appropriate licensed professional.
