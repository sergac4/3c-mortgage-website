PRIVACY POLICY

Your information matters.

Effective date: August 12, 2026

3C Mortgage Group (“3C Mortgage Group,” “3C,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry or form, communicate with us, or request mortgage-related or real-estate-related services.

Important: Do not submit Social Security numbers, full bank account numbers, passwords, credit card numbers, government identification documents, tax returns, bank statements, paystubs, credit reports, or other sensitive financial documents through ordinary website contact forms, email, or unsecured chat. If sensitive documentation is required, we will provide or direct you to an appropriate secure method.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of our website, and information received from service providers or other parties involved in a requested transaction or service.

  • Contact information: name, email address, telephone number, mailing address, and similar information.
  • Inquiry and transaction information: loan purpose, property location, estimated property value or purchase price, requested loan amount, occupancy or property-use information, timing, and other details you choose to provide.
  • Application and financial information: when you proceed through an authorized secure application or loan process, information may include employment, income, assets, liabilities, credit information, transaction history, and other information reasonably necessary to evaluate or process a mortgage or related request.
  • Website and device information: IP address, browser and device information, referring pages, pages viewed, timestamps, and similar technical or usage data.
  • Communications: information contained in emails, calls, text messages, forms, chats, and other communications with us.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • respond to inquiries and provide requested services;
  • evaluate, process, facilitate, or service mortgage and financing requests;
  • communicate about applications, transactions, appointments, documents, and service updates;
  • provide information about mortgage, real estate, financing, or related services where permitted by law and consistent with applicable consent requirements;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and business systems;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or other unlawful activity;
  • maintain business, compliance, licensing, and transaction records; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, audit, reporting, and enforcement obligations.

3. How We Disclose Information

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information as reasonably necessary to provide requested services or operate our business, including to lenders, investors, loan origination or processing providers, credit and verification providers, title or escrow companies, appraisal providers, insurance providers, technology vendors, communications providers, professional advisers, regulators, government authorities, and other parties involved in a transaction or required by law.

Service providers are expected to use information for the services they provide and subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

4. Mortgage Applications and Sensitive Financial Information

A website inquiry is not a mortgage application, loan approval, commitment to lend, or guarantee of terms. If you decide to proceed with a mortgage application, additional information and disclosures may be collected through a secure application, loan origination, lender, or verification system. Information submitted through those systems may also be governed by the privacy notices and terms applicable to those services.

5. Email, Telephone, and Text Communications

If you provide your telephone number or email address and consent to communications, 3C Mortgage Group may contact you regarding your inquiry, requested services, transaction, or other communications permitted by law. Depending on the consent you provide, communications may include calls, emails, or text messages and may use automated technology.

Consent to marketing communications is not a condition of obtaining mortgage services. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of marketing text messages by replying STOP. You may request help by replying HELP or contacting us using the information below. Opting out of marketing communications does not prevent communications that are legally permitted or reasonably necessary to service an existing transaction or respond to a request.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and service providers may use cookies, pixels, local storage, analytics, or similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, understand site usage, measure performance, maintain security, and improve our services. Browser settings may allow you to block or delete certain cookies, although doing so may affect site functionality.

7. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights regarding personal information under applicable California law. Depending on the law and circumstances, these may include rights to request information about personal information we collect, request access or correction, request deletion subject to legal exceptions, and receive information about certain disclosures or uses.

Mortgage and financial-services information may also be subject to federal or state financial privacy laws and exemptions. Certain information must be retained to comply with mortgage, lending, licensing, tax, fraud-prevention, recordkeeping, or other legal obligations.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and as required or permitted by applicable mortgage, financial-services, licensing, tax, recordkeeping, litigation, fraud-prevention, and other laws. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the nature of the relationship or transaction.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use secure channels provided for sensitive financial or identity information.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may link to or integrate with third-party websites, application portals, scheduling tools, lenders, service providers, social platforms, or other services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review those policies before providing information.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, services, or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
